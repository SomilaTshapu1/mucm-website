Foundation

Our Priorities

Key Priorities & Objectives

MUCM exists to:

  1. Effective Local Government
    Provide effective local government representation in the Makana municipal district.
  2. Essential Services
    Restore and maintain water, electricity, sanitation and refuse removal.
  3. Infrastructure
    Rehabilitate and maintain roads, public buildings and utilities.
  4. Economic Development & Jobs
    Promote economic development and job creation within Makana.
  5. Responsible Public Finances
    Ensure transparent and accountable financial management of municipal resources.
  6. Education
    Foster educational excellence and support Rhodes University and local schools.
  7. Safety & Security
    Promote public safety and community security.
  8. Environment
    Protect and enhance the environment for current and future generations.
  9. Good Governance
    Combat corruption, maladministration and abuse of power.
  10. Human Dignity & Equality
    Advance human dignity, equality and freedom in every area of governance.

Commitment to Basic Services (Minimum Standards)

  • ◆ At least 25 litres of safe drinking water, free, per person per day.
  • ◆ 50 kWh of electricity, free, per indigent household per month.
  • ◆ Regular refuse collection to all areas, with recycling and waste-reduction programmes.
  • ◆ High-speed internet connectivity and public Wi-Fi in public facilities across the municipality.
  • ◆ Adequate housing and protection from arbitrary eviction.
